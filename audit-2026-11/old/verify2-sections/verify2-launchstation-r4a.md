## 2. Reasoning for every non-VERIFIED verdict

### LAUNCHSTATION-085 — VERIFIED (narrowed version; reasoning recorded because the task required re-derivation)

Byte figure, recomputed from the definitions, not the filed arithmetic:

- `McFatCache` = `int entry[1 + (256 × 2)]` = 513 × 4 B = **2,052 B** (`mcman-internal.h:122-124`). `mcman_fatcache[2][MCMAN_MAXSLOT]` (`main.c:60`): going 10 → 4 removes 2 × 2,052 × 6 = **24,624 B**.
- `MCDevInfo` is annotated and laid out as **384 B** (`mcman-internal.h:298-328`). `mcman_devinfos[4][MCMAN_MAXSLOT]` (`main.c:64`): 4 × 384 × 6 = **9,216 B**.
- Backing `vmc_cardinfo_t` (`mciomanx_backing.c:14-24`): three `int` (12 B) + `s16` + `u16` (4 B) + `int` (4 B) + `u8` + padding = **24 B**; array `[MCMAN_MAXSLOT]` at `:26`: 24 × 6 = **144 B**.
- **Total: 24,624 + 9,216 + 144 = 33,984 B** — the settled figure reproduces exactly.

Slot-population claim: the only slot derivation is `mcman_unit2card` (`mcdev.c:216-231`, 11 call sites, all in `mcdev.c`); its VMCMAN branch is `unit & (MCMAN_MAXSLOT-1)` = `unit & 9`, image {0,1,8,9}. No in-tree flow presents a unit above 1: the EE mounted-state table is `int vmcMounted[2]` (`src/filer.c:60`), `filerMountVmc` rejects `slot > 1` (`src/filer_browser.c:2352`), enumeration and `vmcN:` re-parse gates are all bounded at 2, no `"vmc2:"`–`"vmc9:"` literal exists in `src/` or `include/`, the feature documentation promises only vmc0:/vmc1: (`docs/changes.txt:258`), and the only other in-tree mcman-library consumer, smbd, imports physical **xmcman** (`iop/smbd/imports.lst:18-23`). Slots 2–9 (and, in-tree, 8/9 as well) therefore cannot be populated by any in-tree flow. The narrowed claim survives.

### LAUNCHSTATION-088 — VERIFIED-DRIFT (count 11 → 10)

Every cited site was read: each loop copies in 16-byte `u32[4]` chunks bounded by `while (pfsentry < pfseend)` with `pfseend = pfsentry + sizeof(McFsEntry)` (or the PS1 struct), i.e. exactly `memcpy` semantics, including the two reverse-direction sites in `ps2mc_fio.c` (1021-1027, 1060-1066, which copy `pcache → pfsentry`). But a complete census — grep for the `*((u32 *)…)` chunk pattern and for every `+= 16` loop terminator across `iop/vmcman/src/*.c` — yields **5 sites in `main.c` and 5 in `ps2mc_fio.c`, 10 total**. The finding's own location list also enumerates only 10 ranges (4 + 1 in `main.c`, 5 in `ps2mc_fio.c`) while its headline says 11. No eleventh site exists in `ps1mc_fio.c` or elsewhere in the tree. Substance (duplication, size-multiple-of-16 hazard) stands; the count and the "~110 lines / net −100 lines" sizing derived from it are overstated by one site.

### LAUNCHSTATION-089 — VERIFIED-DRIFT (count correct, location list incomplete)

A grep for the exact computation `temp = (x + 1) - temp` returns 5 sites in `main.c` (2667, 2887, 2926, 2963, 2987) and 4 in `ps1mc_fio.c` (188, 240, 532, 704) — the 9 ranges the finding lists. The claimed total of 11 is nevertheless correct: the same idiom occurs twice more in `main.c` under different variable names, at **`:3115-3131`** and **`:3149-3165`** (`temp2 = j + 1` / `temp2 = i + 1`; `temp1 &= ~7; temp1 = temp2 - temp1;` then `mce[index]->wr_flag |= 1 << temp1;`), which the location list omits. The arithmetic identity also checks: for `x + 1 ≥ 0`, `(x+1) − ((x+1) & ~7) = (x+1) mod 8`, and the surrounding sign scaffolding only matters for inputs below −1, which the dir-index callers (−1..14) do not produce. Corrected citation set: the 9 filed ranges plus `main.c:3115-3131` and `main.c:3149-3165`.

### LAUNCHSTATION-093 — VERIFIED-DRIFT (disconnect lines wrong)

The guard asymmetry is exactly as filed: `scsi_cmd` checks `scsi == NULL` at `scsi.c:78` and `scsi_cmd_read_capacity16` at `scsi.c:129` (both commented "unplugged during start-up"), while `scsi_cmd_rw_sector` (`scsi.c:139-176`) dereferences `scsi->queue_cmd` with no priv check and `scsi_read`/`scsi_write` read `scsi->max_sectors` from `bd->priv` without re-checking it between chunk iterations. The drift is the disconnect citation: priv is nulled in **`scsi_disconnect` at `scsi.c:406-417` (`bd->priv = NULL` at `:416`)**, not at the filed `:426-431` — those lines are inside `scsi_init`. Exploitability remains INFERRED (depends on BDM disconnect serialisation, not established in-tree), exactly as the finding itself states.

### LAUNCHSTATION-100 — REFUTED (live-finding framing; the dead code's internal defects are real but unreachable)

Three independent defeats, any one of which removes the filed reachability:

1. **Not compiled.** `usmb2_readdir` is wholly inside `#ifdef USMB2_FEATURE_OPENDIR` (`iop/smb2man/usmb2/usmb2.c:743-820`). The `smb2man` target that compiles `usmb2.c` (via `smb2man.c`, per `ps2.yaml:246-258`) defines only `USMB2_FEATURE_WRITE`, `USMB2_FEATURE_NTLM`, `USMB2_FEATURE_UNICODE`, `USMB2_FEATURE_STAT`, `USMB2_FEATURE_MKDIR`, `USMB2_FEATURE_UNLINK`, `USMB2_FEATURE_RENAME` (`ps2.yaml:262-271`). `USMB2_FEATURE_OPENDIR` is defined nowhere in the repo.
2. **No caller.** Tree-wide grep for `usmb2_readdir` / `usmb2_opendir` returns only the definition and the header prototypes (`usmb2.h:93-94`). The 272-byte `dh` buffer the finding posits (`16 + MAX_DIR_SIZE`, `usmb2.c:69,740`) is likewise only ever allocated on the OPENDIR create path, which is itself compiled out.
3. **The live path does the checks.** Directory listing in the shipped driver is `smb2man_querydir` / `smb2man_dread` in `iop/smb2man/smb2man.c:1209-1290`, a separate implementation that clamps the reply length to `SMB2MAN_DIRBUF` (`:1232`), clamps `namelen` to the remaining copied bytes, and bounds the `next` chain step before advancing (`:1275-1285` region) — the opposite of the finding's "most server-exposed operation in the driver" characterisation.

Inside the dead function the cited absences are textually real (unbounded `memcpy(&dh[16+4], …, u32)` at `:792`, unchecked chain walk at `:795-817`), so the text could support a Cat 1 dead-code note on the vendored OPENDIR block. As filed — a live Cat 6 server-controlled heap defect — it is refuted.

### LAUNCHSTATION-102 — VERIFIED-DRIFT (several line ranges off by a few lines)

### LAUNCHSTATION-105 — WEAKENED (ynDialog proven; nonDialog has no exceeding caller)

The `ynDialog` half survives in full: `strcpy` into `char msg[512]` at `src/gui.c:20`, and two caller families can exceed it by construction — the language-load error report built in `char tmp_s[4096]` at `src/lang.c:537-564` (its `-2` branch interpolates a `filePath` of up to `MAX_PATH`, putting the report well past 512 bytes for a long path) and the installer confirmations built in `char msg[MAX_PATH + 96]` at `src/main_exploit_installer.c:1004,2487` (a near-`MAX_PATH` interpolated path alone exceeds 512).
