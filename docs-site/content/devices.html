Why Not checked is selectable

A demand-loaded driver cannot know whether hardware is absent until it runs. A Not checked device therefore remains navigable; opening it triggers the existing lazy loader. A confirmed Absent card can be displayed but is skipped by focus navigation.

USB units

USB is labeled USB / MASS. The implementation discovers available units and does not treat mass0: as a permanent identity.

USB enumeration and intermittent stalls

When the USB stack starts, Launchstation probes at most four times with spaced checks, including late second-drive discovery. Each probe uses a synchronous PS2 IOP filesystem RPC: the number of retries is bounded, but a single blocked driver call cannot be timed out safely by the EE. The controller's analog light going out can indicate a stalled PAD/SIO2 or IOP session; it does not, by itself, prove a software reset. An overloaded USB power rail is also possible with two bus-powered drives. Compare behavior with one drive, a powered hub, and the older launcher baseline. Do not hot-unplug a drive during active writes.

Hide unavailable devices

When unavailable-device display is disabled, those cards are removed from both drawing and navigation indices; the focus rectangle does not pass through hidden/disabled cards.
